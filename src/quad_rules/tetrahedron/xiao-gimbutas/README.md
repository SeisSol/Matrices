# Xiao–Gimbutas rules for the tetrahedron

Rules with positive weights and points inside the tetrahedron, exact up to degree 1 to 15, from

H. Xiao, Z. Gimbutas, A numerical algorithm for the construction of efficient quadrature rules in two and higher
dimensions, Computers & Mathematics with Applications 59(2), 663–676, 2010,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.camwa.2009.10.027

Except for degrees 1 and 5, the rules are not invariant under permutations of the vertices.

The data are taken from the online encyclopedia of quadrature rules, https://quadraturerules.org,
files `rules/Q000002/tetrahedron-*.rule` of https://github.com/quadraturerules/quadraturerules (commit 6d86927).
The values are unchanged; the files here only use a different layout
(the weight as fifth column instead of after a `|`) and add a header.
